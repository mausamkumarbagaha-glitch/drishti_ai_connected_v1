Personal settings

Keep these settings private. Do not enter bank PINs, passwords or other secrets.

Online services: Google Maps for navigation links; OpenStreetMap community data via public Overpass instances for nearby places; Tesseract.js for in-browser text recognition. External services can be slow or unavailable. No continuous tracking is performed.

Not a replacement for a white cane, guide dog, orientation-and-mobility skills, or careful road-crossing practice. Never cross a road solely because AI or this app says it looks clear.
